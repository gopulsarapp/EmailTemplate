How Growing Smiles Dental Centre Streamlined Daily Operations

A practical example of how a growing dental practice improved communication workflows and reduced front-office complexity with Pulsar.

Featured Practice
The Growing Smiles Dental Centre
Growing Smiles Dental Centre integrated Pulsar into its daily front-office operations to support a more connected and efficient patient communication workflow.
The Challenge

As patient activity increased, the front-office team needed to manage scheduling, communication, call handling, and administrative coordination more efficiently while maintaining a positive patient experience.

The Solution

Pulsar was implemented as an automation layer alongside existing systems, helping centralize communication workflows and streamline daily operational tasks through integrations with Open Dental, QuickBooks, and VoIP platforms.

The Result

The practice improved communication efficiency, reduced administrative burden, and created a smoother workflow for both team members and patients.

✓ Reduced front-desk workload
✓ Better scheduling coordination
✓ Fewer missed communication opportunities
✓ More efficient daily operations
